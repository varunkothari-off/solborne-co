# Solborne Sovereign Desk — design rationale

Hero borrows teenage.engineering's full-bleed object-as-hero move — the box
itself is the interaction, no carousel chrome — and its two-step type scale
(one oversized name, one small mono spec line). The compliance section
borrows Vanta's badge-wall-as-trust-signal, remapped to RBI/DPDP/ICAI as a
live status strip instead of legal prose. Motion intent follows hellohello's
Awwwards SOTD "AI in Design Report 2026": one continuous scroll-driven state
change (the dim-the-room LED sequence), not triggered pop-ins.

Self-audit: no cream/serif/terracotta, no near-black+acid-green, no purple
gradients, glassmorphism, blobs, stock AI imagery or emoji. One signature
move only — the unplugged-cable object; brass stays reserved for LEDs, CTAs
and the single live-status dot. Warning red appears exactly once. Every
section closes on a concrete fact, not a restatement. Mobile hero + CTA land
inside the first scroll.

Full reference notes (type scale / grid / signature interaction per source,
and the note on why live scraping was blocked this session) are in the
canvas's own annotations; see the published design.
