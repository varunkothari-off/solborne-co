# Offer clarity and services

24 October 2026. The owner can navigate the payment scenes but still cannot explain the offer. Requested design-thinking/expert UI redesign, a Services page, complete clarity of work and expected outcomes. Existing checkpoint: source0410e3d, staging388e2719. This revision continues the approved staging-only review.

## Customer job and design decision

An unfamiliar owner should understand what Solborne can do, what they receive, what costs money and what they must decide before being asked to explore or sign in. The previous campaign-first entry answers how one payment workflow behaves, but omits orientation and services navigation.

Use an overview homepage → Services → optional labelled example. Keep fixed scenes in the payment example. Main reference pages are normal documents with direct links; users can scan and choose their depth. Reject a mandatory story as the only introduction, and reject a bare feature catalogue without jobs/outcomes. This is a design hypothesis, not a measured comprehension/conversion result.

## Acceptance

- AC-01: Homepage first screen explicitly identifies websites and business automation; visibly separates paid scoped work and free first one-page static website files; direct Services navigation on desktop/mobile without a demo or sign-in. Reused illustration, no custom animation.
- AC-02: Services states customer job, work/deliverable, intended outcome, owner decisions, dependencies/exclusions, price/timing approach and what happens after enquiry. Payments/documents/quotations are illustrative scoped examples; no implication of live connected products or established customer results.
- AC-03: Free offer is one responsive static page, editable source and handover guide. Private reviewed preview within24h of confirmed brief; publication requires approval. Domain/hosting/maintenance/extra pages/automation separate. Paid scope/price/timing/third-party and ongoing costs, acceptance and support terms agreed before work. No invented prices, guarantees or percentages.
- AC-04: Stable descriptive navigation and a branded footer; compatible old pages gain Services links. How-it-works and contact copy support both routes. The payment example is labelled near its entrance and links back to Services. No-JS access, keyboard focus, readable mobile type and360px layout; no new motion.
- AC-05: Local preview and staging share the same entrance; local preview is loopback-only/read-only and cannot submit or connect live services. Baseline and previous payment/story URLs remain accessible. Staging isolation, auth, models, private data and production deployment unchanged.

## Sources and claim boundaries

Existing offer.html, terms.html, operations.html and specs/007-jtbd-walk define scope. Current implementation prepares briefs and review handoffs; it does not establish live integrations or measured business outcomes. No fixed paid rate, support duration or SLA has been approved. Explain proposal-specific terms rather than inventing them. Describe the connected status/next-action view before using Business Digital Twin; no autonomous-business claim.
