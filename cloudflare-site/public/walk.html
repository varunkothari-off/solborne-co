03 / STORY & CANVAS

Start with what you know.

Required details not filled yet

Share information you are comfortable making public. Do not include customer records, identity documents or confidential financial details. Story answers are processed on submission; only the website facts you approve are stored.

Keep this page open while you work. Unfinished answers are not saved if you leave or reload it.

These prompts help you prepare. They do not automatically fill your website. Short answers are enough; use “already covered” when your first answer covers a follow-up.

QUESTION 01 / 05

What do you do, and who is it for?

Describe the work, the people you serve and what led you here. Use your own words.

QUESTION 02 / 05

What are you proud of in your work?

Describe something you do carefully or differently. Keep it specific and true.

QUESTION 03 / 05

How does your work help a customer?

A real example helps. Leave private customer names out; do not invent a result.

QUESTION 04 / 05

What can you share to support your story?

Mention public work samples or official links, and what should stay private. “No public proof yet” is a valid answer. These notes are not added to your website automatically.
