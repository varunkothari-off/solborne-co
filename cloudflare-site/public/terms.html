Website terms

The scope of the current free website journey. Updated 13 October 2026.

The current service

Register, complete the guided Walk and approve your website brief. Your request is processed and privately reviewed before files are released. Submission does not mean that a website is instantly approved, released or published online. After you submit your confirmed Walk of Fame brief, we send your private, manually reviewed website link within 24 hours. Timelines for additional work and revisions are agreed separately.

What you receive

The current free offer is a responsive one-page website and its editable HTML source, plus a README. Hosting, a domain, maintenance and additional work outside the website-copy editor are separate. Released files are downloaded through your authorised browser access. See the offer details.

Your content and permissions

Supply accurate business facts and only content, images and contact information you have authority to publish. Review the draft and approved facts carefully. Your private registration email is not automatically made public.

Future services and payment

Your released static website files are lifetime-free to keep and use. Hosting, a domain and ongoing services are separate; completing the Walk does not activate them. Any paid work, hosting, domain transfer, revision scope or ongoing support needs separately agreed terms. This website does not collect payment or promise leads, sales or search rankings.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site, submit unlawful content, attempt to obtain another person’s files or share private access material. Your retry receipt is private and does not create a permanent account.

Design attribution and questions

The current Solborne design uses original layout, illustrations and motion, with the MIT-licensed Bootstrap foundation and licensed fonts. Earlier archived designs and some generated website templates use HTML5 UP and retain their required attribution. Third-party material has its own licence. For enquiries or complaints, use our contact page. These terms do not exclude rights that applicable law gives you.
