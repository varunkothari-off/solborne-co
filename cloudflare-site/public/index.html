Before you begin

A few practical answers.

What is free?

The PDF and editable business canvas are completely free to unlock after verified sign-in. Your agreed first static website files and editable source are also free to keep after our team’s review. Hosting, a domain, maintenance and paid automation are agreed separately.

Why is the first website free?

We want good businesses to have a place online. Your first website lets you see what we can do. If you want more, we agree that work separately. No upgrade required.

Will my website be published instantly?

No. Your request is processed and privately reviewed before file release. Completing the Walk does not automatically publish your website or reserve a customer subdomain. Any hosting arrangement is agreed separately.

Can I begin if I already have a website?

Yes. Tell us about your current website and what you would like to improve. The initial offer remains the same free one-page V1 scope; a larger redesign or automation is separately scoped.

What happens after I sign in?

Share a few details, then take the Walk of Fame. We use your story to shape your website. You can also unlock the free business canvas whenever you want space to think.
