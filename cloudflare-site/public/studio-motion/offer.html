Offer, hosting & revisions

Two distinct stages. One clear boundary.

Current free V1 journey

The existing Solborne offer includes a responsive one-page website, editable HTML source and README after processing, private review and release. Hosting, a domain, maintenance and additional work outside the website-copy editor are separate. There is no payment step in that journey.

Proposed next pilot

  • A static portfolio website with a hosted preview.
  • Seven days beginning with first draft delivery.
  • One consolidated revision for colours, images and wording.
  • Paid scope for additional automations.

These are proposed pilot terms. Automated delivery and expiry are not implemented here. Permanent hosting, domain transfer, source ownership/licensing, retention after expiry, delivery timings and any paid design-support limits need explicit agreement.

Paid work, cancellation and refunds

This preview accepts no purchases or payments. No refund or cancellation policy for a paid service is asserted here. Before paid work begins, its scope, price, taxes where applicable, schedule, acceptance, cancellation and refund terms must be provided and agreed. Mandatory consumer rights cannot be removed by these terms.

Future automation

Enquiry routing, follow-up and acquisition workflows are planned examples, not implemented services on this preview. No advertising return, ranking, sales or lead-volume result is promised.
