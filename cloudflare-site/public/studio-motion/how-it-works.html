How it works

One clear step.
Then the next.

Understand your business. Create its first website. Decide what would help it work better.

  1. 01

    Free canvas

    Start with a clearer picture.

    Sign in with Google to unlock your canvas. Download the PDF or editable text and put your business, customers, offer and priorities in one place. Use it at your own pace.

    Sign in to unlock canvas
  2. 02

    The Walk of Fame

    Tell us about the real business.

    Save a short enquiry, then take the Walk. We explore who you serve, what makes you worth choosing and the biggest problem worth solving. Bring your existing website and brand assets if you have them.

    Your answers guide audience, marketing and industry design research. We choose a direction that fits your business.

  3. 03

    Lifetime-free website files

    Review a website that feels like yours.

    We prepare a responsive static website and manually check the content, design, links and disclosures. You approve the public facts before release. The agreed website files and editable source are free to keep.

    Submission does not publish a website instantly. Hosting, a domain and maintenance are separate arrangements.

  4. 04

    Optional paid improvements

    Only build what earns its place.

    If there is a useful next step, we show you the workflow, intended benefit, price and where a person stays in control. Optional paid concepts in your private preview are clearly marked and inactive.

    You can keep your free website without buying an upgrade.
