The Walk of Fame

Understand the business.
Then decide what to build.

A conversation about your customers, your work and the improvement that matters most. A website is the first useful expression of that understanding.

Start with the real business.

Who pays you, for what, and why do they choose you? We map how a customer finds you, gets in touch, buys and receives your service. Existing websites, brand assets and public evidence give us a starting point.

Agree the biggest actionable pain point.

What gets delayed, repeated or missed? We consider its impact, frequency, the information available and what success would look like. We distinguish a confirmed problem from a hypothesis. Automation is proposed only when it helps.

Research before design.

We research your audience, marketing, relevant digital workflows and industry templates. Layouts are assessed against your brand, content, visitor needs and commercial-use rights. Solborne’s own design is not the template for every business.

Make the free website useful on its own.

Your free V1 is a complete responsive page with editable source, within the current agreed scope. Your private review preview clearly separates included features from optional paid proposals. Proposed paid features are labelled, inactive examples; they cannot accept bookings, payments or enquiries.

Keep people in control.

Our team verifies the website before file release. You approve your public facts, design direction and any paid scope before publication or activation. Private operational notes stay separate from approved website content.

Choose the next improvement.

If the research identifies a useful next step, we explain the workflow, the expected benefit, what still needs proof, its price and the human decisions it preserves. You can keep the free deliverable without buying an upgrade.

Start today.

The existing brief editor is available. The guided AI interviewer is prepared in source but its Gemini connection has not been activated or live-tested. It will not claim research, design or automation has happened before those steps are completed.
