The story behind our logo

A bearing.
Not the steering wheel.

Our mark begins with a simple idea: a guide helps you read the road. You are the one driving.

The amber point is a fixed reference to steer by. The level horizon stands for a calm, honest reading, whether the news is good or bad. The line connecting them is the guide helping you take that reading.

The point of light is the “sol”; carrying that clarity forward is the “borne”. Plain and warm by design, it should feel equally welcoming to a five-person firm and a five-hundred-person firm.
