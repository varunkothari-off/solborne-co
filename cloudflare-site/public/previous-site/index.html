A few things
worth knowing.

Clear boundaries before you start.

What do I receive through the current free journey?

The V1 website build and editable source are free. Your brief is processed and privately reviewed before file release. Register here to, approve your brief and check the released files.

Is hosting included?

Hosting and a custom domain are separate from the current free-source offer. The seven-day hosted preview is a proposed next pilot; registration does not activate it.

What becomes public?

The business facts, images and public contact links you approve. Your registration reply email is not automatically published. Read the current enquiry notice before submitting.

What does the proposed free revision cover?

Colours, images and wording in one consolidated round. Automations and paid design support are agreed separately.

What happens after the proposed seven days?

Keeping the site, hosting and domain transfer will be discussed before the preview ends. Those commercial and delivery terms still need to be finalised.

How this enquiry is handled

Your name, email contact, enquiry type, and permission to respond are saved in Solborne’s separate Cloudflare registration store. Solborne reviews them through its authenticated Cloudflare account. They are not added to another brand’s list or a marketing subscription.

Receipt identifiers, submission times, consent wording version, and temporary security checks support processing and abuse prevention. No documents or business records are requested. Only the public website facts you explicitly approve are stored when you complete the Walk. Your private registration email is not automatically added to the website.

For questions about your saved details or to request a correction or removal, contact varun@getsolborne.com.
