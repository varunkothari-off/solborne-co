Website terms

The scope of the current free website journey. Updated 13 October 2026.

The current service

Register, complete the guided Walk and approve your website brief. Your request is processed and privately reviewed before files are released. Submission does not mean that a website is instantly approved, released or published online. No delivery date is promised unless separately agreed.

What you receive

The current free offer is a responsive one-page V1 website and its editable HTML source, plus a README. Hosting, a domain, maintenance and additional work outside the website-copy editor are separate. Released files are downloaded through your authorised browser access. See the offer details.

Your content and permissions

Supply accurate business facts and only content, images and contact information you have authority to publish. Review the draft and approved facts carefully. Your private registration email is not automatically made public.

Future services and payment

The seven-day hosted preview, consolidated design revision and automation services described on this site are proposed or planned. They are not activated by registration. Any paid work, hosting, domain transfer, revision scope or ongoing support needs separately agreed terms. This website does not collect payment or promise leads, sales or search rankings.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site, submit unlawful content, attempt to obtain another person’s files or share private access material. Your retry receipt is private and does not create a permanent account.
