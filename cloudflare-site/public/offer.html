A useful first website demonstrates how we understand your business. Further work earns its place by addressing a need you have confirmed.

Included in the current free V1

One responsive static page, its editable HTML source and a README, after brief processing, manual team review and release. Your brand, audience, services, public contact route and applicable disclosures shape the page. Research informs the design; findings and unverified assumptions stay distinct.

The free page stands on its own. Hosting, a domain, maintenance, additional pages and separately agreed automation are outside that free scope. No payment is required to submit the free brief.

Optional paid improvements

We may recommend enquiry qualification, follow-ups, bookings or another digital workflow when the Walk and research support it. These are possible services, not a claim that those integrations already operate here. Every proposal states the problem, intended outcome, required access, human approval points, implementation scope and separately agreed price.

What you see in a private customer preview

Included free features work normally. Optional paid concepts are labelled “Optional paid upgrade · Not active” and remain disabled. They illustrate a relevant possibility; they do not capture information or simulate success. Prices that are not agreed are marked “Quoted separately”. Unpurchased proposals are removed from the final public website.

Solborne’s own website uses normal working navigation and enquiry routes rather than disabled upgrade demonstrations. A live link is not evidence of an implemented booking, payment or automation system.

Human review before delivery

Submitting a brief does not automatically publish a website or reserve a customer subdomain. We review the facts, design, assets, disclosures and working links before release. You approve the public content. Delivery dates and any hosting arrangements are confirmed separately.

Paid scope, cancellation and refunds

This website has no checkout. Before paid work begins, its scope, price, taxes where applicable, schedule, acceptance, cancellation and refund terms must be provided and agreed. Mandatory consumer rights cannot be removed by these terms. No sales, lead-volume, advertising-return or ranking result is guaranteed.
