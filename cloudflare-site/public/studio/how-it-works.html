The Walk of Fame

From your story
to your website.

A thoughtful process. Clear decisions. You stay in control of what represents your business.

01

Tell us what you do.

Who do you help? What do they need? We capture your services, audience and the character of the business. You can start from an existing website.

02

Choose what belongs online.

Confirm public contact details, operator identity, permissions for your assets, and the relevant service and privacy notices. Your private registration email stays private.

03

Give the story a visual direction.

A relevant layout, considered type and your actual brand. The free editor currently creates a single-page draft; additional pages and design work are separately scoped.

04

Review before release.

Check the public facts and preview, then submit for private review. Source files are available after release. Submission does not automatically publish a website.
