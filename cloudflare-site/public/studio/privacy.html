Privacy & data handling

This notice covers solborne.in, its guided Walk and the improvement-brief tool. Updated 14 October 2026.

Who handles your information

Solborne is operated by KISNO PROPTY LLP. Contact varun@getsolborne.com for questions about your information, correction, removal or withdrawal of permission to respond.

Registration and your website brief

We save your name, private email contact, enquiry type and permission to respond in Solborne’s separate Cloudflare registration store. They support your enquiry and access to the guided Walk. They are not added to another brand’s list or a marketing subscription. Receipt identifiers, submission times, consent wording version and temporary security checks support processing, recovery and abuse prevention.

When you complete the Walk, we save the answers and public website facts you approve for processing and private review. Only the approved public facts and contact options go into the released website. Your registration reply email is not automatically published. Do not send confidential customer records or information about others without authority.

Storage, access and service providers

The site and registration store run on Cloudflare. Its hosting infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser information and operational logs. Solborne reviews saved enquiries through its authenticated Cloudflare account. Temporary browser cookies provide access and security checks; they do not create a permanent account. Keep your private retry receipt private. The file alone cannot grant access.

Keeping and removing information

Saved enquiries and briefs remain available for processing, private review and access to released versions. Automatic deletion of saved requests is not currently implemented. A fixed retention schedule has not yet been established. To request correction, removal or withdrawal of permission to respond, email the contact above and identify your request without sending your private retry key. We may need to confirm that the request is yours before changing private records.

Guided AI Walk

The optional guided Walk sends your business answers and confirmed draft facts to Google Gemini only after you agree to AI processing. Solborne saves the conversation, draft, consent version and review notes privately in Cloudflare D1 so you can return to your progress. Do not submit customer financial records, identity documents or confidential contracts. No document-upload facility is provided. Google processing is subject to the connected API project and its terms; paid-tier configuration is required before activation.

You can delete your saved AI conversation in the guided Walk. Previously approved website versions and separate registration records are retained under the existing version/release process. A fixed automatic deletion schedule is not active. A temporary website-editor handoff places your chosen public facts in browser tab storage; it expires for import after one hour and is removed on import. AI research and marketing reviews are advice and do not certify legal compliance or publish a website. Contact Solborne regarding correction, withdrawal or deletion requests.

Improvement-brief tool

The tool processes your website URL, role and goal in your browser to prepare a draft. It does not inspect your website, send an enquiry or persist the draft in browser storage. Reloading clears it. Copying puts the draft on your clipboard; sharing happens only when you paste or send it yourself.

Third-party links and local assets

Fonts and design assets are served with this website. No marketing analytics or payment collection is added by this design. External websites operate under their own terms. Contact details and the registered office are available on our contact page.

Private presales records

Solborne saves enquiry details, contact permission, website briefs, AI Walk progress and website-version status in Cloudflare D1 and a separate private Solborne schema in our Supabase presales database. Access is restricted to our authorised team and server-side intake service. Deleting an AI Walk also removes it from the next database sync; enquiry and website-version records remain subject to the existing privacy and retention policy. Contact us to request removal of those records.
